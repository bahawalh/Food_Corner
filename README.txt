FOOD CORNER - LAB 3 CSS

Folder structure:
FoodCorner/
├── index.html
├── css-concepts.html
├── box-model.html
├── overflow-display.html
├── style.css
└── images/
    ├── food-pattern.svg
    ├── pizza.svg
    ├── burger.svg
    ├── sandwich.svg
    └── pasta.svg

HOW TO RUN:
1. Keep all files exactly in this structure.
2. Double-click index.html, or right-click it and open with Chrome/Edge.
3. Use the links on the page to open the three separate demonstration pages.
4. Do not move the HTML files away from style.css or the images folder, because the relative paths depend on this structure.

LAB REQUIREMENTS COVERED:
- Inline, internal and external CSS
- Element, class, ID, attribute, grouping and descendant selectors
- :hover and :first-child
- ::before and ::after
- Cascade, specificity and inheritance
- Font, text, color and background properties
- Box model and box-sizing
- Margin collapsing demonstration
- Width/height and min/max constraints
- overflow visible, hidden and scroll
- display block, inline, inline-block and none
- Borders, radius, shadows and spacing
- Four required food cards and contact form
